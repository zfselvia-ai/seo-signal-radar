ChatGPT Search massively adopted site: operator fan-outs on Aug 8; Reddit citations fell 86% | ChatGPT搜索8月8日起大规模使用site:限定查询，Reddit引用量暴跌86%P1Data-backed
为何重要 Why: OpenAI正在用'官方域名优先'的粗暴信任过滤替代开放网页检索，非官方站点在ChatGPT Search中的可见性急剧下降，GEO策略需重新校准。
证据 Evidence: Promptwatch data: site: searches jumped from 0.4% to ~17% of background queries on Aug 8; Reddit citations fell from 3.83% to 0.52% between Aug 14-17 per Search Engine Land/SEJ reporting on Promptwatch and Suganthan analysis.
影响 Affects: publisher / ecommerce / SaaS
行动 Action: 测试你的品牌是否出现在ChatGPT的品类查询中；确保官网在site:查询下能直接提供价格、规格等关键信息；监控第三方评测和媒体报道中的品牌提及
如何测试 Test: Ask your core category question 5 times in ChatGPT Search, note which brands appear; then run site:yourdomain.com for key product pages and check if pricing/specs render in plain HTML without JS dependency
衡量 Measure: Brand mention rate in ChatGPT Search responses for target category queries; site:yourdomain.com crawl success rate for key pages
成本与风险 Effort: Medium effort (2-4 hours audit); low risk
Evidence · 2 sources
Promptwatch tracked the site: operator spike in real-time. The 0.4%→17% jump occurred in a single day (August 8). Reddit's citation share inside ChatGPT Search dropped from 3.83% to 0.52% between August 14-17 — an 86% decline. Multiple independent observers (Lily Ray, Suganthan, Search Engine Land) confirmed the correlation. The mechanism appears to be: ChatGPT first generates a search naming brands, then runs site: lookups on each brand's own domain for pricing/specs. This privileges .gov, .org, and established brand domains while excluding forums and lesser-known publishers. Caveat: the 'official domain' guess is sometimes wrong, hitting parked sites.

为何重要 Why: AI搜索引用具有高度不确定性，品牌无法依赖单一优化点；需建立多触点的AI可见性策略。
证据 Evidence: TheSocialDude study of 14,472 AI citations across 1,487 local search queries; Gemini source match rate ~40% for identical questions.
影响 Affects: local / all

Evidence · 1 source
Ben Fisher (TheSocialDude) analyzed 14,472 AI citations across 1,487 local search queries. Key finding: when asked the same question, Gemini's cited sources matched only 40% of the time — indicating high variability in AI search citations. Second finding: a business's own website was the most frequently cited source, suggesting on-site control remains significant. The study focused on local search queries; generalizability to non-local queries is unclear. Marie Haynes contributed a quote, lending practitioner validation.

证据 Evidence: SEOFOMO State of AI Search Optimization Survey, 80 responses in 3 days: expanding content for questions/topics/intents 48%, restructuring content 43%, improving crawlability/accessibility 40%, structured data 26%.

Survey of 80 SEO practitioners conducted by Aleyda Solis/SEOFOMO community. Self-reported activities with 'meaningful positive impact' on AI Search visibility. Still useful as directional signal of what's working in the field.
