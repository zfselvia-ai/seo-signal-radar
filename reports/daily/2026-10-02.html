AI Overviews on 82.9% of US desktop branded SERPs (Sep 29), up from 61% in July; in 76% of cases the AIO sits at position 1 above the brand | 9/29 美国桌面端 82.9% 的品牌词 SERP 有 AIO（7 月为 61%），76% 的情况下 AIO 排在品牌官网之上P1Data-backed
为何重要 Why: 品牌词是最后一块“安全流量”。AIO 占据第一位并引用维基百科（71.9%）、YouTube、LinkedIn 等第三方来描述你的品牌，意味着品牌叙事的控制权在外移，品牌词 CTR 需要单独监控。
证据 Evidence: dataset: 232.8M US desktop SERPs Jul–Sep 2026 (92.5M branded) + 100 Interbrand brand queries × 14 weekly checks (Ahrefs)
影响 Affects: ecommerce / SaaS / publisher / all
行动 Action: 在 GSC 里为品牌词建单独的正则筛选，按周看 CTR 与点击；用 AIO 对你品牌词的回答核对维基百科、LinkedIn、YouTube 上的品牌信息是否过时或错误。
Evidence · 1 source
Ahrefs (Ryan Law with data scientist Xibeijia Guan) analysed 232.8M US desktop Google results from July to September 2026 (92.5M branded, 140.2M non-branded keywords). AIO presence on branded SERPs rose month over month from 61.3% to 65.1% to 73.3%, reaching 82.91% on Sep 29, versus 73.5%→78.3% for non-branded. In a panel of 100 Interbrand brand names, AIOs went from 5/98 (Jul 1) to 63/100 (Sep 30), 76.2% of them at position 1. Most-cited external sources: Wikipedia 71.9%, YouTube 38.6%, LinkedIn 22.8%; only 2 of 58 answers mentioned competitors. No click or CTR impact is measured; US desktop only; Google changes AIO triggers often.
