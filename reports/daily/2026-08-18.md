## Research & Retrieval

- **Study: ChatGPT names brands in its own search queries before fetching anything — 21 of 27 first queries included brands the user never typed | 研究：ChatGPT 在检索前就已决定候选品牌，27 次首轮查询中 21 次带入用户未提及的品牌**  `P2` `Observed`
  - 为何重要 / Why: 如果模型先从训练记忆里拉出品牌清单、再用 site: 探针去验证，那么只盯“被引用的 URL”做 GEO 是把结果当原因。品牌是否进入模型的先验，才是第一道门。
  - 证据 / Evidence: observation: 57 conversations / 3,554 fetched pages from one ChatGPT Plus account (Suganthan Mohanadasan), shared by @randfish
  - 影响 / Affects: SaaS / ecommerce / all
  - 行动 / Action: 测试：针对你的品类问 ChatGPT 10 个“best X”类问题并抓取其 search_queries，记录你的品牌是否出现在模型自己生成的查询里；没出现就先做品牌提及（第三方评测、对比页），而不是优化被引用页面。
  - 详情 / Detail: Suganthan Mohanadasan read the search_queries field in ChatGPT's network responses on one logged-in account (Dubai, Jul 24–25, 2026). 21 of 27 first queries already named brands; brands that appeared in ChatGPT's own query reached the final answer 68.9% of the time (n=119) versus 2.1% for pages that were merely fetched (n=51). Only 110 of 3,554 retrieved pages (3.1%) were cited. The author calls every percentage directional: one account, personalised results, a software-heavy question mix; he also sells Keyword Insights. Rand Fishkin's framing: citation-analysis research may be misleading practitioners.

## Playbook: Worth Testing

- **Playbook: isolate Google AI Mode traffic in Search Console by classifying conversational query fragments | 玩法：在 Search Console 里通过识别对话式查询片段拆出 AI Mode 流量**  `P2` `Observed`
  - 为何重要 / Why: GSC 不单列 AI Mode，但 John Mueller 确认 AI Mode 的追问会以普通查询形式进入报表，所以长句、带代词、多轮式的查询片段可以当作 AI Mode 流量的代理指标。
  - 证据 / Evidence: practitioner method (Suganthan Mohanadasan), four extraction approaches compared; shared by @screamingfrog
  - 影响 / Affects: all
  - 行动 / Action: 导出近 90 天 GSC 查询（API/BigQuery），用正则或分类器标出对话式片段（≥6 词、含 I/my/should/which 等），按周看其展示与点击占比。
  - 详情 / Detail: The write-up compares four ways to pull conversational fragments out of GSC — Glenn Gabe's API+Excel approach, a regex method, a browser extension, and the author's own classifier combining deterministic rules with a fine-tuned xlm-roberta-base model trained on his labelled queries plus synthetic rows in 8 languages — handling up to 100,000 unique queries per run. Hard limit stated by the author: on his property 57.7% of impressions sat in Google's anonymised query pool over 59 days, so every method undercounts; no precision/recall figures are published. The tool is free; large jobs route to his agency.
  - 如何测试 / Test: 导出 GSC 查询 → 规则/分类器打标 → 对话式片段占比按周跟踪，与 AI Mode 在你市场的上线日期对照。
  - 衡量 / Measure: 对话式片段的展示/点击占比及其周环比；若上线后占比上升且 CTR 明显不同于普通查询，说明分割有效。
  - 成本与风险 / Effort: 中：需要 API/BigQuery 导出与脚本；风险是匿名化查询（作者站点占展示的 57.7%）导致系统性低估。
