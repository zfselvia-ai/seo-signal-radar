AI Search / GEO

SearchPilot simulation: across 12 LLM shopping conversations the first-named brand won only once; final picks spanned 4 brands | SearchPilot 模拟：12 轮 LLM 购物对话中首个被提及的品牌仅赢 1 次，最终选择分散到 4 个品牌P2Observed
为何重要 Why: 如果多轮对话会推翻首轮答案，那么基于固定 prompt 首答的“AI 可见度分数”量的是错的东西。这也解释了为什么各家 AI 追踪工具互相矛盾。
证据 Evidence: experiment (simulated): 12 runs × 2 categories, Gemini via API, LLM-as-shopper (Will Critchlow, SearchPilot)
影响 Affects: ecommerce / SaaS / all
行动 Action: 评估 AI 追踪工具时要求多轮对话采样而非单 prompt 首答；自己测时用 3–5 轮带追问的脚本重复 10 次，看推荐品牌的分布而不是“第一名”。
Evidence · 2 sources
Will Critchlow had Claude build a persona from a one-line goal, a small local LLM 'shop' by conversing with Gemini, and Claude analyse the transcripts — 12 runs each for running shoes and garden furniture. Shoes: Brooks was named first in 6 runs but chosen in 1; Saucony was never chosen when named first yet was the most frequent final pick (5). Furniture: Wayfair was named first 3 times and won 0; only 2 of 12 journeys ended where they began. Critchlow states this is a toy model, not real users: n=24, one engine, no statistics. SearchPilot sells testing that competes with prompt trackers.
