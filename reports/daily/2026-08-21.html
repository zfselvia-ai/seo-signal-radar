Evidence · 1 source
Google's Aug 20 announcement, retweeted by Dawn Anderson, lists the changes: a Preferred Sources button to save favourite websites, feedback controls to see more or less of specific topics and links in the Discover feed, and related tweaks across Search, News and Discover. It is the product announcement itself, not a measurement — Google gives no data on how preferred-source status changes impressions or clicks.
