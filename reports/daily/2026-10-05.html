Official Explanations

Helpful-content doc adds a 'main content' definition and four rater attributes — Effort, Originality, Talent/skill, Accuracy; unsupervised bulk gen-AI text = 'little to no effort' | 帮助文档新增“主要内容”定义与四项评估属性：投入、原创、才能/技巧、准确性；无人工监督的批量 AI 文本 = “几乎零投入”P1Confirmed
为何重要 Why: 质量评估指南的语言第一次大段进入面向站长的官方文档，且与 10/1 的 AI 内容指南、虚构作者警告同一周落地。文档不是算法，但它定义了下一次更新会惩罚什么。

影响 Affects: publisher / SaaS / all

Evidence · 3 sources
The doc now states that rater guidelines treat main-content quality as 'one of the most critical factors for assessing page quality', defines main content in five categories (primary text/media, interactive features, user-generated contributions, tabbed/expanded sections, titles and headings) and lists four attributes raters assess: Effort, Originality, Talent or skill, Accuracy. It says feed-generated or unsupervised bulk generative-AI text 'represents little to no effort' and that YMYL content must match expert consensus. Google announced no ranking-system change. Search Engine Roundtable and SEOFOMO surfaced the edit; Marie Haynes notes the 'filler' language has long been in the QRG. Cited sources are the X posts that surfaced it.

影响 Affects: ecommerce / publisher / SaaS

Evidence · 1 source
A second-hand summary of a Google presentation at Search Central Live Deep Dive Barcelona (Sep 30–Oct 2, 2026): when Google does not know a URL's quality or popularity, crawl demand is estimated from the aggregate of its parent path, then the parent's parent, and so on. The Google speaker and exact wording are not in the tweet and no documentation page states this; it is consistent with Google's crawl-budget guidance but should be treated as a conference remark, not a spec.

Playbook: Worth Testing

Playbook: audit Merchant Center image coverage — one global retailer is missing ~8M product images in its feed (2M+ products, average 1 image each) | 玩法：审计 Merchant Center 商品图覆盖率，一家全球零售商 feed 里缺约 800 万张图（200 万+商品，平均每个仅 1 张图）P2Observed
为何重要 Why: 免费商品列表与 AI 购物面都吃 feed 里的图片属性；站上有图、feed 里没图是最常见也最容易修的差距，直接影响商品卡的展示资格与点击。
证据 Evidence: case: ecommerce audit shared by Brodie Clark (@brodieseo), Oct 5
影响 Affects: ecommerce
行动 Action: 导出 Merchant Center 商品数据，统计 additional_image_link 为空或仅 1 张图的商品占比；优先补齐头部 SKU 的多图，并观察免费列表的展示与点击。
如何测试 Test: Merchant Center 导出 → 统计每商品图片数分布 → 对 Top 1000 SKU 补齐 3+ 张图 → 对比 4 周免费商品列表展示/点击。
衡量 Measure: 免费商品列表（Shopping 标签 + 搜索）展示与点击的变化；补图 SKU 与未补图 SKU 的对比。
成本与风险 Effort: 低到中：feed 规则或开发对接；风险是图片不合规（水印、文字）被拒登。
Evidence · 1 source
Brodie Clark describes a store with 2M+ products in Merchant Center where the average image count per product is 1 and each country feed carries about 116K products including variants; multiplied across markets the gap is roughly 8 million images that exist on site but not in the feed. A single audit observation with no uplift measured; the mechanism (additional_image_link populating product cards and visual surfaces) is documented by Google.
