SERP & Algorithm Signals

AI Overviews launched in France Jul 22; 963 top domains show −3.4% CTR overall and a −23.1% median for highly exposed sites | 法国 7/22 上线 AIO：963 个头部域名整体 CTR −3.4%，高曝光站中位数 −23.1%P1Data-backed
为何重要 Why: 少见的带对照组的 AIO 上线前后数据。展示量不动、点击下滑是典型模式，短信息类查询被吸收最多。对还没上线 AIO 的市场，这就是可预期的量级。
证据 Evidence: dataset: aggregated GSC data, 963 of the top 1,000 FR domains, 28 days before vs 9 days after (Ahrefs)
影响 Affects: international / publisher / all
行动 Action: 在 GSC 里用 AIO 触发率把查询分成高/低曝光两组，比较上线前后 CTR；对高曝光的信息类查询，优先改写为能被引用的直接答案 + 独有数据，而不是争排名。
Evidence · 1 source
Ahrefs (Juliette Begue with data scientist Xibeijia Guan) compared daily GSC averages for 28 days before the Jul 22, 2026 launch with 9 days after, across 963 of the top 1,000 French domains by clicks. Highly exposed domains (>20% of queries triggering an AIO) lost a median 23.1% CTR and 82.2% of them lost CTR; a low-exposure control group (<2%) gained 2.3%, giving an adjusted −24.8%. Panel-wide medians were −5.7% CTR and −8.8% clicks; 48% of domains lost over 10% of daily clicks. Caveats the authors state: only 9 post-launch days, AI Mode launched the same day and is not separated out, and −23.1% describes a minority group with no control adjustment.

AI Search / GEO

iPullRank: AI visibility trackers disagree wildly (41% vs 23% for the same brand) — there is no 'accuracy' in AI search tracking, only precision | iPullRank：AI 可见度工具互相矛盾（同一品牌 41% vs 23%），AI 搜索追踪没有“准确”只有“精度”P2Observed
为何重要 Why: AI 答案本身是概率性的，不存在可对标的真值；两个工具的差异不说明谁对。能用的只是同一工具的重复测量稳定性（精度）和趋势方向。
证据 Evidence: expert analysis (Mike King, iPullRank) with an illustrative client case
影响 Affects: all
行动 Action: 选 AI 可见度工具前先做精度测试：同一 prompt 集连续 5 天重复跑，看结果波动幅度；之后只比较同一工具内的趋势，不跨工具比绝对值。
Evidence · 1 source
The piece starts from a client running two AI visibility platforms side by side that reported 41% and 23% presence for the same prompt set. The argument: because LLM outputs vary run to run and with personalisation, there is no ground truth to be 'accurate' against; the usable property is precision — repeatability of one tool's measurement — so changes should only be compared within a tool and over time. No cross-tool benchmark data is published in the article; iPullRank is an agency selling AI-search services.
