Profound: the median AI citation loses half its share within 11 days of peaking; 78% of pages decay within 14 days (883K pages, 7 engines, 12 months) | Profound：AI 引用在达峰后 11 天内中位数份额减半，78% 的页面 14 天内衰减（88.3 万页、7 个引擎、12 个月）P3Data-backed
为何重要 Why: 如果 AI 引用是“过期”而不是“累积”的，GEO 就更像新闻发行节奏而不是一次性优化；但方法上的 14 天平滑让 78% 这个数字部分是定义使然。
证据 Evidence: vendor dataset: 883,000 pages, 1.19M page-engine lifecycles, Kaplan-Meier survival (Profound, ~Sep 30); shared by @sitebulb
影响 Affects: publisher / SaaS / all
行动 Action: 监控：对你被 AI 引用最多的 10 个页面记录引用首次出现日和消失日；若多数在两周内消失，把更新频率而非页面数量作为 GEO 的 KPI。
Evidence · 1 source
Profound sampled 883,000 cited pages (1 in 128) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini and Copilot from Sep 2025 to Sep 2026 and modelled 1,188,816 page-engine citation lifecycles with Kaplan-Meier survival on a 14-day trailing share. Median half-life after peak: 11 days; 78% fall to half within 14 days, the rest median 27–29 days; pages cited by 5+ engines survive 5.5× longer at day 56; cross-engine decay correlation is near zero (0.03–0.09). Caveats: the 14-day smoothing mechanically caps burst pages at ≤14 days, share is measured within Profound's own prompt panel, and no author is named. Profound sells the tracking platform.

Playbook: Worth Testing

Playbook: submit every major child sitemap to Search Console individually, not just the sitemap index, to get per-sitemap indexing coverage | 玩法：在 Search Console 逐个提交主要子 sitemap，而不只提交索引文件，以获得分 sitemap 的索引覆盖数据P2Observed
为何重要 Why: 只提交 sitemap index 时 GSC 把所有 URL 聚合成一份报表，看不出是哪个模板/目录没被索引。逐个提交零成本，诊断粒度立刻细到目录级。
证据 Evidence: practitioner tip from large-site audits (Brodie Clark, @brodieseo, Oct 6)
影响 Affects: all
行动 Action: 在 GSC → Sitemaps 里把每个主要子 sitemap（商品、品类、文章…）单独提交；一周后按 sitemap 看“已抓取未索引”占比，定位问题模板。
如何测试 Test: 逐个提交子 sitemap → 7 天后在页面索引报告里按 sitemap 筛选 → 比较各模板的未索引比例。
衡量 Measure: 能按模板看到未索引比例与原因分布；随后针对性修复的模板索引率提升。
成本与风险 Effort: 极低：几分钟操作；无风险。
Evidence · 1 source
Brodie Clark's observation from auditing large sites: when only sitemap-index.xml is submitted, Search Console groups everything into one macro report, so the first bottleneck is aggregate data. Submitting each major child sitemap (products, categories, articles, etc.) individually gives separate Page Indexing views per sitemap. This is a reporting-granularity change, not a ranking tactic; no uplift is claimed.
