Aleyda Solis: local ccTLDs make up 41% of AI citations in Brazil, 38% in the UK and 24% in Spain — and which engine is most local changes by country | Aleyda Solis：本地 ccTLD 占 AI 引用的比例巴西 41%、英国 38%、西班牙 24%，且“最本地化”的引擎因国家而异P2Data-backed
为何重要 Why: AI 可见度不能只测英语/美国。多语言品牌需要在每个市场各自获得本地媒体与评测的提及，一处优化不会自动在其他市场生效；而且每个市场要盯的引擎还不一样。
证据 Evidence: dataset: Aleyda Solis's own prompt library, 4 markets × 10 topics × 3 engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode), Oct 8
影响 Affects: international / all
行动 Action: 多市场品牌：按市场各跑一组 20 个品类 prompt，列出每个引擎的引用域名，找出本地媒体/评测/社区的空缺，交给当地 PR；按国家决定优先盯哪个引擎。
Evidence · 1 source
Aleyda analysed the citations returned by ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode for the same prompt library localised to the US, UK, Spain and Brazil across ten topics. Using ccTLDs as the local indicator (.uk/.es/.br), local domains make up 41.4% of citations in Brazil, 38.3% in the UK and 24.0% in Spain. ChatGPT has the highest local share in the UK and Spain; Gemini leads in Brazil at 51.7% but is lowest in Spain at 16.6%; AI Mode varies least (22.6%–27.5%). Personal finance, home/DIY and automotive have the highest local shares. She notes ccTLDs understate local targeting since many local sites use .com/.org; prompt counts per cell and repetitions are not stated in the thread.

Playbook: Worth Testing

Playbook: measure AI search impact with seven signals when referrers fail — Ahrefs finds ChatGPT + Claude drive 21.7% of its signups, above YouTube | 玩法：引荐数据失效时用七个信号衡量 AI 搜索影响——Ahrefs 发现 ChatGPT + Claude 贡献 21.7% 的注册，超过 YouTubeP2Observed
为何重要 Why: AI 平台剥掉 referrer、用户事后再 Google 品牌，所以引荐流量系统性低估 AI 的作用。用注册调查、品牌词搜索量、直接流量变化等旁证拼出全貌，比等一个准确归因更可行。
证据 Evidence: first-party data + method (Ahrefs: signup survey, Ahrefs Web Analytics, Brand Radar), Oct 6
影响 Affects: SaaS / all
行动 Action: 在注册/询盘表单加“你从哪里听说我们”（含 ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity 选项），同时按月记录品牌词搜索量与直接流量；三者同向变化即可作为 AI 影响的量化证据。
如何测试 Test: 表单加来源问题 → 跑 4 周 → 对比自报 AI 来源占比与 GA4 AI 引荐占比，差值即“暗流量”规模。
衡量 Measure: 自报 AI 来源占比；品牌词搜索量与直接流量的月环比；AI 来源的转化率 vs 自然流量。
成本与风险 Effort: 低：一个表单字段 + 月度表格；风险是自报偏差，需与品牌词搜索量交叉验证。
Evidence · 1 source
Ahrefs' write-up proposes seven signals: self-reported attribution at signup, AI referral traffic, branded search volume, direct-traffic shifts, web mentions, AI-visibility tracking and conversion rate by source. Its own numbers: ChatGPT plus Claude account for 21.7% of Ahrefs signups ('more than one in five'), ahead of YouTube and behind Google; on wordcount.com AI referrals are 92.4% ChatGPT; AI visitors convert to Ahrefs Free at 6%, four times the organic rate. All figures come from Ahrefs' own properties with no sample sizes, and the author concedes nobody can definitively prove AI search value; Ahrefs sells the tools used.
