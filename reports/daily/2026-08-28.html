Evidence · 1 source
Google's post states that from Aug 30, 2026 the impact of a site reputation manual action applies only to results shown to users outside the EEA; for EEA users the affected section 'may be separated' so it ranks independently over time. Google attributes the change to discussion with the European Commission while warning that an overbroad application of the DMA could stop it acting on real threats. Search Console notices and reconsideration requests continue, and 'eligible sites' may take disputes to mediation — neither eligibility nor the mediation process is defined, and no affected-site counts are given.
Google Search Central Blog · 2026-08-27 · official documentation
Update to the Site Reputation Policy. In 2024, we introduced our site reputation policy to stop a practice where third-party content is published on a trusted website just to exploit that site's good reputation to rank higher in Search. This practice hurts search quality, and creates a bad experience for users.
