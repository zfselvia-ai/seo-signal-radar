## Official Explanations

- **Google updates its generative-AI content guidance: 'critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content' — titles, meta, schema and alt text included | Google 更新生成式 AI 内容指南：“发布前必须人工核查所有 AI 生成内容”，含标题、meta、结构化数据与 alt**  `P1` `Confirmed`
  - 为何重要 / Why: 措辞从“可以用 AI”变成“必须人工复核”，并直接引用质量评估指南 4.6.5（scaled content abuse）和 4.6.6（低投入/低原创）。这是在给无人监督的批量生成划线。
  - 证据 / Evidence: official doc: developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content (changelog Oct 1); verified against the live doc Oct 10
  - 影响 / Affects: all
  - 行动 / Action: 检查内容流程里 AI 生成的标题/meta/结构化数据/alt 是否有人工复核环节；没有的先补到发布 checklist，并抽样回查已发布页面的事实错误。
  - 详情 / Detail: The Search Central changelog for Oct 1, 2026 says the gen-AI guide was updated with information from the Search Quality Rater Guidelines. New text explains that models predict a likely sequence of words and 'may contain inaccuracies (also known as hallucinations)', and that it is critical to manually fact-check and review all AI-generated content — explicitly including title elements, meta descriptions, structured data and image alt text — before publishing. The doc cites QRG sections 4.6.5 and 4.6.6. Google frames this as syncing documentation with its developer-event talks and announces no ranking-system or policy change. Cited source: Search Engine Roundtable's report, retweeted by Dawn Anderson.

## SERP & Algorithm Signals

- **AI Overviews on 82.9% of US desktop branded SERPs (Sep 29), up from 61% in July; in 76% of cases the AIO sits at position 1 above the brand | 9/29 美国桌面端 82.9% 的品牌词 SERP 有 AIO（7 月为 61%），76% 的情况下 AIO 排在品牌官网之上**  `P1` `Data-backed`
  - 为何重要 / Why: 品牌词是最后一块“安全流量”。AIO 占据第一位并引用维基百科（71.9%）、YouTube、LinkedIn 等第三方来描述你的品牌，意味着品牌叙事的控制权在外移，品牌词 CTR 需要单独监控。
  - 证据 / Evidence: dataset: 232.8M US desktop SERPs Jul–Sep 2026 (92.5M branded) + 100 Interbrand brand queries × 14 weekly checks (Ahrefs)
  - 影响 / Affects: ecommerce / SaaS / publisher / all
  - 行动 / Action: 在 GSC 里为品牌词建单独的正则筛选，按周看 CTR 与点击；用 AIO 对你品牌词的回答核对维基百科、LinkedIn、YouTube 上的品牌信息是否过时或错误。
  - 详情 / Detail: Ahrefs (Ryan Law with data scientist Xibeijia Guan) analysed 232.8M US desktop Google results from July to September 2026 (92.5M branded, 140.2M non-branded keywords). AIO presence on branded SERPs rose month over month from 61.3% to 65.1% to 73.3%, reaching 82.91% on Sep 29, versus 73.5%→78.3% for non-branded. In a panel of 100 Interbrand brand names, AIOs went from 5/98 (Jul 1) to 63/100 (Sep 30), 76.2% of them at position 1. Most-cited external sources: Wikipedia 71.9%, YouTube 38.6%, LinkedIn 22.8%; only 2 of 58 answers mentioned competitors. No click or CTR impact is measured; US desktop only; Google changes AIO triggers often.
